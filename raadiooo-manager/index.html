Raadiooo Manager

Raadiooo Manager is a private tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Raadiooo to manage his own channel through the YouTube Data API. With the owner's authorization and only on his instructions, it:

  • uploads the owner's own videos and Shorts to his channel;
  • sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails and visibility (private, unlisted or public, always chosen by the owner);
  • uploads and updates caption/subtitle files (e.g. Romanian subtitles) for the owner's own videos;
  • reads the owner's own channel and video statistics.

The tool is operated through a cloud-hosted AI assistant that acts only on the owner's requests (see the Privacy Policy for details).

It is not offered to the public and only accesses the owner's own YouTube account.
